About the Module
----------------

- **Name**: Coffee Chain ERP
- **Technical Name**: `coffee_chain_erp`
- **Version**: 1.0
- **Author**: Nush Ojha
- **Dependencies**: `base`, `crm`, `sale`
- **Features**:
  - Centralized outlet management
  - CRM lead auto-creation
  - Sales integration with service and payment customization

Getting Started
---------------

This documentation will guide you through:

- Creating a new custom model for coffee outlets
- Integrating outlets with CRM for automated lead generation
- Extending Sales Orders to link with outlets, service types, and payment methods

Each section contains clear instructions, sample code, and view configurations to help you replicate or extend the module.
